How the analysis works

nine deterministic stages
  1. Upload
  2. Extract text
  3. Clean
  4. Detect sections
  5. Match skills
  6. TF-IDF
  7. Cosine similarity
  8. Score
  9. Render charts
Upload and validate

The file must be a real PDF under {{ max_size_mb }} MB. It is saved under a random safe name, never exposed publicly, and deleted as soon as the analysis finishes.

Extract and clean the text

pypdf reads the text layer and pdfplumber recovers anything missed. Ligatures, smart quotes, bullets and broken line wraps are normalised.

Detect sections and fields

Headings are matched against standard spellings with regular expressions; email, phone, links, degrees, institutions, years and dates are pulled out with targeted patterns.

Match skills and keywords

Every skill in data/skills.json is matched with a word-boundary aware regex plus an alias map, then TF-IDF surfaces the terms that matter most in this document.

Score, compare and render

Weighted sub-checks produce the four component scores. Cosine similarity compares the resume with the chosen role, and matplotlib renders the charts and radar plots.
